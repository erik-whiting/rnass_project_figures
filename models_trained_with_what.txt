ContextFold: RNA Strand https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2536673/
    * Actual database link: https://rnasoft.ca/strand/
    * Many of the sequences in RNA Strand are found in bp-RNA
ContraFold: RNA Strand 1.3
EternaFold:
    * STRAND-S
        * "same data used to train RNAsoft and CONTRAfold" (CONTRAfold not version 2.0)
    * Cloud Lab datasets: Has a lot of data from RFAM
IPKnot:
    * Validation only, but uses RNA STRAND, pk168, and RFAM 10.0
MXFold:
    * TrainSetA: "Literature Derived from literature Dowell and Eddy (2004); Do et al. (2006); Andronescu et al. (2007); Lu et al. (2009); Andronescuet al. (2010)."
    * TrainSetB: RFAM
MXFold2:
    * Same as MXFold2 but also with RNAStralign
NeuralFold:
    * Same as MXFold
NUPACK:
    * Not trained, based on Mathews et al. 1999 and Lu et al. 2006
        * Additional parameters from Xia et al. 1998 and Zuker 2003
pKnots:
    * Tested on Sprinzl tRNA database, not really trained either
    * Link: https://watermark02.silverchair.com/21-13-3011.pdf
RNAFold:
    * Based on Turner 2004 energy parameters
    * Evaluated on non-pseudoknot RNAs from RNA STRAND
RNAStructure:
    * Same as RNAFold
Simfold:
    * RNA STRAND 2.0
SPOT-RNA:
    * bpRNA subset for initial training
    * transfer learning based on another bpRNA subset
